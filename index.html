🏆 Top picks by use case

Best for Cantonese transcription workflow

Huawei FreeClip 2 (~$180 / ¥1299)

Best balance of comfort + AI smarts + cross-platform. Pairs with any phone, integrates deeply with HarmonyOS (Xiaoyi 同声传译), works with iOS Live Captions, Tinrec, Notely Voice. 9 hr battery, 5.1g each bud, 38 hr with case. Only real con: best AI features locked to Huawei phones; non-Huawei users get ~80% functionality.

Best overall sound quality (open-ear)

Bose Ultra Open Earbuds (~$299)

Still the gold standard for sound in an open ear-cuff. Immersive Audio with head-tracking, aptX Adaptive/Lossless support, 7.5 hr battery. Real users: "rivals some brands' in-ear models" for richness. Downsides: no AI/transcription features, no multipoint BT 6.0, weak mic for noisy calls.

Best value "all-rounder"

Anker Soundcore AeroClip (~$130)

The standout at $130. 5.9g, 12mm titanium drivers, Virtual Bass tech, 4 mics with AI call noise reduction, BT 5.4 multipoint, IPX4, 32 hr total. Real user verdict: "clearest calls in this price tier; missing some premium features but unbeatable value." Beats Huawei FreeClip 2 on price, loses on AI smarts.

Best battery + durable for outdoor

Shokz OpenDots One (~$200)

11 hr earbud / 40 hr with case — best battery of any clip-on. Bassphere dual 11.8mm drivers, Dolby Audio, IP54, BT 5.4 multipoint. Cycling/commuting: "the only clip-on that actually handles wind noise better than bone conduction." Real users note less tactile controls than OpenRun Pro 2.

Built-in AI translation + transcription

Timekettle W4 Pro (~$370) — different form factor

Not clip-on (semi-in-ear), but the only earbud with native Cantonese translation + AI meeting notes built-in. 43 languages, 96 accents, ~0.2s latency, 95% accuracy. Offline translation for 13 pairs. Best for users who value the AI function more than sound quality.

🈶 Cantonese transcription ecosystem

Clip-on earbuds are microphones + Bluetooth. The "Cantonese transcription" capability lives in the phone app, not the earbuds. Below: tools that work with ANY Bluetooth earbud and have solid Cantonese support.

Native (free, on-device)

iOS 26.4 Live Captions (新!)

Released Feb 2026 · iOS 26.4+ · 100% on-device
⭐ 8.5/10

即時字幕 支援 中文(香港). Real-time captions from mic OR app audio. Best new option in 2026 for HK users. Tested by Unwire.hk: Cantonese accuracy "不俗,能夠識別廣東話用字,沒有明顯錯誤". English-brand name transcription is weak (Gemini → "Gemalate").

iPhone Voice Dictation (Cantonese)

iOS 13+ · Built-in · Free
⭐ 7/10

Settings → General → Keyboard → Dictation Languages → 粵語 (香港). Crucial hack: disable all other language dictionaries in Settings → General → Dictionary (only keep 繁體粵語), accuracy jumps from ~85% to ~93% per HK Threads users. Free, offline, fast — best for short messages.

Android Gboard / Samsung Keyboard 廣東話

Android 9+ · Free · Online (Google ASR)
⭐ 7/10

Google's Cantonese ASR is competent for everyday dictation. Requires internet. Less privacy-friendly than iOS native. Better for English-Cantonese code-mixing than iOS in some cases.

Dedicated AI recording apps (Cantonese-optimized)

Tinrec (秒聽錄音)

iOS / Android / Web · Free 100 min/mo · Pro available
⭐ 9/10

Best for the use case you're describing. Independent benchmark: 8.3% CER on 5-min Cantonese radio clip in real office environment. Unique features: AI dialog query ("會議結論係乜?"), auto-generated meeting minutes, to-do extraction, supports YouTube/Bilibili link transcription. Designed for the Asian Cantonese/Mandarin code-mixing use case. Tinrec's own benchmark vs. Otter.ai shows Tinrec wins on Cantonese 100% of the time; Otter doesn't support Cantonese at all.

Notely Voice (offline Whisper)

iOS / Android · Free · Open source
⭐ 7.5/10

100% offline, runs OpenAI Whisper locally on phone. Strong privacy (no cloud). Tested by Unwire.hk on Cantonese: high accuracy, but produces 書面語 (written) output automatically, not literal transcription. Will rewrite 唔使 → 無需. ~465MB model download. Use case: max privacy, or you want formal meeting minutes.

Otter.ai

Web/iOS · Free 300 min/mo · English only
⭐ 2/10 for 粵語

Does NOT support Cantonese or Chinese at all. Best-in-class for English meetings (speaker ID, real-time transcription, Zoom integration), but useless for your use case. Skip unless your meetings are pure English.

Notta

iOS / Android / Web · 50+ languages
⭐ 5.5/10

Claims 50+ languages including Cantonese, but real-user reports: Chinese accuracy is significantly worse than Tinrec. Use only for short clips where you can verify.

ListenUp Dictation

macOS / iOS · Paid · 97 languages
⭐ 7/10

Live dictation (not file upload), 97 languages including Cantonese, smart mode produces cleaned-up text (removes filler, understands self-corrections). Mac/iOS integration is the best in class. Not a meeting recorder — for typing into any text field.

Translation earbuds (built-in, not iOS-bound)

Timekettle W4 Pro (mentioned)

Semi-in-ear · ~$370 · 43 langs incl. 粵語
⭐ 8.5/10 for translation

Only earbud with native Cantonese in translation list. ~0.2s latency, 95% accuracy. Has its own AI Memo, meeting minutes, transcription. Caveat: not clip-on (semi-in-ear) — different comfort category. If translation matters more than form factor, this is the only choice.

FIIL GS Links AI

Hook ear · Mentioned in passing
⭐ 7/10

AI conference assistant built-in: 7m pickup radius, 98% accuracy claim, supports 川/粵/滬 dialects in transcription. Real-time translation 中↔英. Worth a look but Chinese brand, less verified in Western reviews.

🥇 My recommended Cantonese workflow

For HK / Greater Bay users with ANY Bluetooth earbud:

  1. Live meeting / class: iPhone → iOS 26.4 Live Captions (新功能, 100% on-device, private) + Tinrec running in background for searchable transcript + meeting minutes
  2. Quick voice notes / WeChat reply: iPhone 內建 Cantonese dictation (after the dictionary hack above)
  3. Offline / privacy critical: Notely Voice (Whisper local)
  4. Cross-language conversation: Tinrec translation OR Timekettle W4 Pro (if budget allows)

Skip these (real-user feedback)

  • Bose Ultra Open at full price ($299): Incredible sound but no AI features + weak mic + no wireless charging. Only worth it on sale (~$199).
  • Anker Liberty 5 Pro Max for Cantonese: Has the best on-device AI note-taker, but the in-ear form factor + poor outdoor mic + heavy case make it a niche choice. Only buy if you specifically need UK-GDPR-friendly local transcription AND have no other transcription workflow.

Test before you buy — the form-factor variable

Open-ear / clip-on fit is highly ear-dependent. Real-user feedback shows ~20-30% of people find some clip-on uncomfortable after 2+ hours, regardless of brand. If possible:
  • Buy from a retailer with good return policy (Amazon, JD, Anker.com, Huawei Store)
  • For HK: try Huawei/Samsung/Sony stores for FreeClip 2 / Soundgear demo
  • Japanese users (Kakaku) consistently say: "必ず試聴出来るお店で装着を試してから、購入することをお勧めします" — always try before you buy
